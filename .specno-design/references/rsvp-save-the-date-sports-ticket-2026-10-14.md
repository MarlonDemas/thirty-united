# References: rsvp-save-the-date-sports-ticket

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Product context:** "Thirty United" is a one-page RSVP save-the-date for Marlon "Makes" Demas's 30th birthday (Monday 21 December 2026, venue TBA). Guests are mostly South African friends and family on phones, many not football fans. The job to be done is to open the invite, RSVP, verify an email code, and keep a personalised ticket stub.
**Pattern under research:** single-page invite/RSVP flow with a ticket reveal (marketing-style single page, mobile-first, WCAG 2.2 AA).
**Stack profile:** `web-vite` (web family).
**Capture method:** Playwright chromium, 1440x900 and 390x844, first-load viewport only, accessed 2026-10-14. Screenshots are stored for internal reference only; they are third-party work and are not to be redistributed or shipped.

## Cross-Reference Synthesis

**Gap noted:** none of the nine captured pages shows a ticket or stub, a code-verification step, or the post-RSVP state. The ticket moment is therefore not grounded in a shipped reference here. Two searches (a general web search and an Awwwards event listing) turned up no usable ticket-metaphor site; this file does not invent one. Follow-up: browse Awwwards/Godly for a ticket or pass reveal, or capture a club digital ticket.

### Patterns appearing in 3+ references (the category median: risk of slop)

- Invitation or content shown as a portrait card with a soft drop shadow: Evite, Paperless Post, Luma (square tiles). **Decision for this project:** diverge in form (a landscape match ticket with perforation) but keep the "one object" idea.
- Big centred or left-aligned headline plus one pill CTA in the first viewport: Partiful, Luma, Paperless Post, Posh, Evite. **Decision:** follow, but the CTA is the tear/tap action on the ticket.
- Sans-serif UI with a single expressive display face: Partiful, Mundial, Posh, Copa90, Luma. **Decision:** follow; pair a condensed display with a plain body.
- Dark, photo-led hero: Partiful, Copa90, Man Utd. **Decision:** diverge for the default; offer a non-photo version so no football imagery is required.
- Category/template grids and shop CTAs: Evite, Paperless Post, Mundial. **Decision:** diverge; one invite, no grid.

### Patterns appearing in 1 reference (potential signature moves)

- Chat-bubble overlays as social proof (Partiful only). **Decision:** evaluate as a Signature candidate (guest names and fun facts appearing as bubbles).
- 1px black ruled boxes like a printed programme (Mundial only). **Decision:** evaluate as a Signature candidate; the ticket outline becomes the rule.
- Broadcast lower-third with a tag chip and ticker bar (Copa90 only). **Decision:** evaluate as a Signature candidate for the "TBA venue" status line.
- Tabular numerals in a row layout (Premier League only). **Decision:** use for ticket data fields.
- Serif headline with a single red action (Man Utd only). **Decision:** evaluate; it is the lightest way to nod to United without the crest.
- Envelope-liner reveal (Paperless Post, Evite). **Decision:** reinterpret as a ticket-sleeve reveal.

### Aesthetic tone clusters

- **Editorial print culture:** Mundial, Paperless Post, Man Utd (serif). Most likely fit because it signals "football" and "premium" while staying readable for non-fans.
- **Stage-lit nightlife:** Partiful, Copa90. Possible if pivoting toward a party-night feel; risks the "club-night promo" and loud-motion problems.
- **Airy product-minimal:** Luma, Posh. Possible if the ticket is meant to be the only expressive element on a very quiet page.
- **Seasonal mass-market:** Evite. Anti-reference (confetti, balloons, "30" in gold foil).

3. *"The thing that makes this unmistakable in a screenshot is one oversized condensed all-caps '30' in the display face on an otherwise empty page, with nothing else competing for attention,"* anchored by Posh (single huge type line on white) and Partiful (one heavy headline voice).

## Anti-Slop Audit

No Dribbble or Behance concept work was used; all nine references are shipped production sites. Patterns that appear in shipped references here and should still be treated as AI-cliché or category-median tells:

- Gradient headline word ("start here"): appears in 1 shipped reference (Luma); avoid.
- Soft-shadow floating cards with rounded corners: appears in 3 shipped references (Luma, Evite, Paperless Post); a ticket must differ in silhouette.
- Confetti, balloons, gold-foil numerals: appears in 1 shipped reference (Evite); an explicit anti-reference.
- Official club crest as decoration: appears in 2 shipped references (Man Utd, Premier League); excluded by the brief (trademark).
- Heavy glitch or auto-playing motion on first load: appears in 1 shipped reference (Copa90); avoid, and honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
