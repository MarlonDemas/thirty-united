## Pattern under research

A calm, plain (non-game) invitation start page. Guests read the essentials (who, what, when, where), then choose between playing the 3D match-day game and a quick plain RSVP. Today the game is the landing experience and "Just reply" is a small link top-left. The owner wants the plain invitation first and the game offered as a choice.

## Questions the references must answer

1. How do plain invite pages lay out who, what, when, where and RSVP?
2. How do they present a primary and a secondary path (RSVP versus details or add to calendar)?
3. How does a mode choice ("play it" or "just reply") sit without demoting either option?
