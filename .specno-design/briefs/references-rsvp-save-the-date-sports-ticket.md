# Brief: references for rsvp-save-the-date-sports-ticket

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Stack profile:** web-vite (web family)
**Product:** Thirty United, a one-page RSVP save-the-date for Marlon "Makes" Demas's 30th birthday. Date: Monday 21 December 2026. Venue: TBA.
**Audience:** guests on phones via one shared link, mostly South African friends and family, many not football fans.
**Theme:** sports, football first; Makes is a loud-and-proud Manchester United fan. Texture: minimal and premium, but fun.

## Pattern under research

Single-page invite/RSVP flow with a ticket reveal. The save-the-date arrives as a match ticket the guest tears or taps open. They RSVP (Yes/Maybe/No, name, team or other sport, fun fact, invite channel), verify their email with a code, and get a personalised ticket stub back.

## Surface tier

Marketing-style single page, mobile-first, WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Direct competitors used

Partiful, Luma (lu.ma, now luma.com), Paperless Post, Posh, Evite.

## Adjacent references used

Mundial, Manchester United (manutd.com), Premier League, Copa90.

## Anti-references

- Betting-app UI.
- Cartoon birthday-invite templates (confetti, balloons, gold-foil "30").
- Club-merch-store feel and wall-to-wall crests.
- The official Man Utd crest (trademarked); do not rely on it.
