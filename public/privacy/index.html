Privacy notice: Makes's 30th birthday RSVP

I'm Marlon Demas, and I'm the responsible party for the details you give on this site, under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). I collect only what I need to manage RSVPs for my 30th birthday on Monday 21 December 2026 and to send you event details. I use Convex, Netlify and Gmail to run the site. I delete your details within 30 days after the event, by 20 January 2027. You can ask me to show, correct or delete your details at any time at demasmarlon@gmail.com.

What I collect

It depends on how you reply. Replying is optional.

If you say Yes or Maybe, I collect:

  • your first name and surname, and the name you'd like on your shirt, if different (optional)
  • your email address
  • your sport, and which one if it's not football, and your team
  • whether I should send the details by email or WhatsApp, and your WhatsApp number if you pick WhatsApp
  • whether you're bringing someone, and their name if you give it (optional)
  • a ticket number I assign to your reply (ticketNo), and the time you first replied and last changed your reply
  • the version of this notice you saw when you replied

If you give me a plus-one's name, I hold it on their behalf, so please only share a name you're comfortable sharing.

If you say No after replying before, I collect your name and email only. If you earlier said Yes or Maybe and then change to No, your earlier answers are replaced, so I no longer keep your sport, team, WhatsApp number, plus-one or shirt name. I keep your ticket number and the time you first replied.

If you say No without having replied before, I collect your first name and surname, plus a short reason if you choose to give one. I don't ask for your email, and I keep the time of the reply and the notice version.

To limit abuse, I also keep keyed hashes of your email or name for rate-limiting. They can't be turned back into the email or name.

Why I collect it

To manage the guest list and send you the event details. I don't use it for anything else, and it isn't shared beyond the three services listed below.

Who handles it for me

I use three services. They process your details for me and only to run the site.

  • Convex stores the RSVPs and runs the site's back end. It holds everything listed above.
  • Netlify hosts the pages you load. It doesn't receive your RSVP answers; those go from your browser straight to Convex.
  • Gmail (Google) sends your email code. It sees your email address, the code and the text of the email.

These services are based outside South Africa, so your details are processed outside South Africa (POPIA section 72), under each provider's own data-protection terms.

Email codes

To check your email address, I send you a six-digit code.

  • I store only a hash of the code, never the code itself.
  • The code expires after 10 minutes.
  • Until it expires, I hold your email address next to the hash.
  • An hourly job deletes expired codes.

What's stored on your device

There are no cookies and no analytics on this site.

The only thing the site saves on your device is one setting, stickSide, which remembers whether you put the phone joystick on the left or the right. It holds no personal details.

How long I keep it

I delete your details within 30 days after the event, by 20 January 2027.

Your rights and who to complain to

Under POPIA, you can ask me to:

  • show you the details I hold about you (access)
  • correct them (correction)
  • delete them (deletion)
  • stop using them (objection)

Email me at demasmarlon@gmail.com.
